Delete your account

Lord of the Banner: Viking Age · Max & Jax Studios

Last updated: 6 October 2026

If you signed in to the cloud save (with Google Play Games or with Google), you have an account for the game. You can delete it, with its cloud save, at any time.

In the game (the quickest way)

  1. Open the game and tap Cloud save on the title screen.
  2. Tap Delete my account, under the buttons.
  3. Confirm: sign in with Google once more, or tap to confirm if you're signed in with Google Play Games.

Without the game

Email max.jax.studios@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Lord of the Banner account”, from the Google account you signed in with. If you signed in with Google Play Games, tell us your gamer name. We'll delete your account and its cloud save within 30 days, and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account for the game, and every way in linked to it (Google Play Games, Google)
  • The cloud copy of your campaign

Both are gone for good: we keep no other copies.

What stays

  • The campaign saved on your device, until you uninstall the game or start a new campaign
  • Your Google account and your Play Games profile, which are Google's and aren't touched
  • What Google AdMob collected to show ads, which is held by Google: reset your advertising ID in your device's settings

Only the cloud save?

In the game, Cloud save → Delete cloud save removes the cloud copy and keeps your account.
