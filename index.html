Privacy Policy

Lord of the Banner: Viking Age

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version. Your campaign is saved on your device. If you sign in (with Google Play Games on Android, or with Google), a copy of it is kept in our cloud save so you can carry on from another device. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: now and then one after a battle, and others only when you choose to watch one for a reward. On Android, if you're signed in to Google Play Games, your achievements, leaderboard scores and game stats go to your Play Games profile. We don't use analytics, we never ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone, and we never sell your information.

1. Who we are

Lord of the Banner: Viking Age (“the Game”) is made and published by Max & Jax Studios (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game handles, why, and the choices you have. It covers the Game on Android and in a web browser.

2. What we never collect

  • Your precise (GPS) location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera
  • Payment details: if the Game ever offers a purchase, Google Play handles it and we never see your card

We don't sell or rent your information to anyone, and the Game contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own.

3. Your campaign on your device

The Game keeps these on your device: your campaign (your warband, silver and food, the state of the map, your quests and the kings' favour, and your deeds toward achievements), your settings, a random code that tells your devices apart in the cloud save, which of your achievements and scores have already gone to Play Games (so each is sent once), and how long you've played and when the last ad was shown (so ads stay spaced out). Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in Android's settings removes them.

4. Signing in and the cloud save (optional)

The cloud save keeps a copy of your campaign so it survives a lost phone and carries on from another device. You don't have to use it: the Game plays fully without signing in.

How you sign in

  • Google Play Games (Android): if you're signed in to Play Games on your phone, the Game signs you in to the cloud save by itself. If you sign out in the Game (Cloud save → Sign out), it won't sign you in again until you choose to.
  • Google: you can also sign in with your Google account; it's the way in on the web or a PC.

What is stored

  • Your sign-in account, kept by Google Firebase Authentication: a random user ID and the sign-in you used. For Play Games, your Play Games player ID and gamer name; for Google, your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture, as Google provides them.
  • The cloud copy of your campaign, kept in Google Cloud Firestore under your user ID, with a short summary used to compare copies between your devices: the date in the game, your title, men, silver and renown, when it was saved, your device's random code and the kind of device it came from (for example “an Android phone”).

Only you can reach your cloud save: the database lets a signed-in player read and write their own save and nothing else. We use it only to keep your campaign. It is never used for advertising, never shared and never sold. It stays until you delete it (section 10).

5. Ads: Google AdMob

The Game is free and paid for by ads. Now and then an ad shows as a battle hands you back to the map: never in your first quarter hour of play and at most one every five minutes of play. Other ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward (doubling your plunder, the monks tending your wounded, your scouts finding outlaw camps). When you're offline, there are no ads.

Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (and from it an approximate location), information about your device (such as its model and Android version) and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, and, with your consent where the law requires it, to personalise them.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before ads are personalised. You can change them at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy choices for ads.
  • Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for teens.
  • You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your device's settings.

Read more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

6. Google Play Games Services

On Android, the Game uses Google Play Games Services to sign you in. While you're signed in to Play Games, it also sends Play Games:

  • Achievements: each one you earn, and your progress toward those that count up.
  • Leaderboard scores: the most foes you've felled in one battle, your best combo, your renown, and how many days you took to win a crown. Other players see them with your Play Games gamer name, as your Play Games settings allow.
  • Game stats: each campaign battle as it ends (whether you won, the foe, how many fought and fell on each side, the foes you felled, your best combo, how long it lasted, the ground and the season), each deed the Game counts (such as men hired, bands broken or requests answered), and your progress (renown, title, honour, the kings sworn to you, your forts, the men you lead and the campaign's day). Play Games keeps lifetime stats from them on your profile and uses them for its quests and challenges.

These go to Google, not to us. Your Play Games profile, what it shows and who can see it, is Google's and is handled under the Google Privacy Policy; you can change its visibility, or delete it, in the Play Games app or your Google account.

7. Permissions

  • Internet: for ads, signing in and the cloud save.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described above.

The Game asks for nothing else: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13: it's a game of battles between armies (without blood). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we'll delete the account.

9. Security

Everything the Game sends travels encrypted (HTTPS). Your account and cloud save are stored by Google Firebase, protected by Google's security and by database rules that let only you reach your save. We keep no other copies.

10. Deleting your data

  • In the Game: Cloud save → Delete cloud save removes the cloud copy and keeps your campaign on your device. Delete my account removes your sign-in account and its cloud save for good, after you confirm.
  • Without the Game: see Delete your account. Email us and we'll delete your account and cloud save within 30 days.
  • On your device: uninstall the Game, or clear its data in Android's settings.
  • Ads: what AdMob collects is held by Google. Reset your advertising ID in your device's settings, and see Google's policy above.

11. The services we use

  • Google AdMob: ads
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): signing in and the cloud save
  • Google Play Games Services: signing in on Android, achievements, leaderboards and game stats

That's all: no analytics, and no other advertising or tracking tools.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Important changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes on Google Play.

13. Contact us

You see all of it and can change or remove any of it before you send. We use what you send only to answer you and to make the Game better; ask and we'll delete it.
